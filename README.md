# DIWALI: THE FESTIVAL OF LIGHTS

Storyboard, narration and AI video prompts | KBC office event (looped playback)
Total: about 2 min 47 sec per loop (about 2 min 52 sec with a 5-second music-only gap)

FLOW AT A GLANCE
- Hook: 0:00 - 0:28 (28 sec), Photorealistic. Title card, office, ignition, reveal.
- History: 0:28 - 1:38 (70 sec), Animated. Rama, Krishna, other beliefs, why it matters.
- Map: 1:38 - 1:46 (8 sec), Animated to real. India lights up region by region.
- Regions: 1:46 - 2:22 (36 sec), Photorealistic. North, west, south, east.
- Closing: 2:22 - 2:47 (25 sec), Photorealistic. Common threads, the room, loop fade.

STYLE ANCHORS (add to the end of every prompt)
[P] Photorealistic, cinematic documentary look, 35mm lens, warm golden-hour lighting, shallow depth of field, natural skin tones, authentic Indian setting, no text, no logos, no subtitles.
[A] Hand-painted 2D animation, storybook illustration, rich orange, gold and deep indigo palette, soft glowing lamplight, gentle parallax motion, silhouettes only, no faces, no text.
Negative prompt: text, watermark, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed faces, modern fireworks logos, blurry.

MAKING IT WORK ON LOOP
- People join mid-video: 3-second title card at the start of each loop: "Diwali: The Festival of Lights".
- Noisy room, possibly muted: Burn in English subtitles; let the visuals carry the story.
- Narration repeating constantly: Keep music soft and narration calm, or add a 5-second music-only gap between loops.
- Event-specific lines: No "today we invite you" wording. The closing is timeless.
- Seamless restart: Final frame (dimming diya) matches the opening frame (match strike, diya ignites).

PRODUCTION NOTES
- Clip lengths: Most tools give 5-10 sec per generation. For clips with 3-4 beats, generate in one go if the tool allows, or split into two 5-sec clips and join them in the editor.
- Dense narration: Clips 8 and 9 carry the most words; give them 11-12 sec if the voiceover feels rushed, taking the time from the map transition.
- Consistency: Use the same seed or a reference image across clips of the same style. In photorealistic clips, prefer wide shots, hands and silhouettes, since AI faces change between clips.
- Sacred symbols: Keep deities and the temple as silhouettes and symbols only. If a tool renders the names badly, delete the names from the prompt; the visual description is enough.
- Audio: Generate silent clips. Add narration (e.g. ElevenLabs) and soft instrumental sitar or flute music in your editor (CapCut, Canva).
- Subtitles: Burn in English subtitles for the noisy, possibly muted room.
- Review: Have an Indian colleague check the history clips (5-11) and regional clips for cultural accuracy before the event.
